This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how it is used, and what choices you have. We keep data collection to a minimum: the calculator itself does not collect or store the values you enter.

This information may be processed through server logs and related infrastructure for website delivery, security, troubleshooting and service reliability.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use these types:

  • Consent and preference cookies: remember the choice you made in the cookie message, so we don't ask you every visit.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may mean you are asked for your consent again.

You can change or withdraw your consent to non-essential cookies and similar technologies at any time by using the Privacy Settings or Cookie Settings link available on this website.
