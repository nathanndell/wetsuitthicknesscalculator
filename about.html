About Wetsuit Thickness Calculator

A free wetsuit thickness calculator, with no sign-up.

This website helps surfers choose the right wetsuit thickness based on water temperature. Enter the water temperature, select Celsius or Fahrenheit and get an instant recommendation. Below the calculator itself includes additional guidance on accessories such as boots, gloves and a hood.

People use it to choose a wetsuit before heading out for a surf, check whether their current wetsuit is suitable for the conditions, prepare for surfing trips or decide when to switch between summer and winter wetsuits.

How the calculation works

  • The calculator uses the water temperature you enter to recommend a wetsuit thickness based on general surfing temperature guidelines.
  • Warmer water generally requires a thinner wetsuit, while colder water requires thicker neoprene to help retain body heat.
  • The calculator supports both Celsius and Fahrenheit, converting the temperature where necessary before working out the recommendation.
  • Each temperature range corresponds to a recommended wetsuit thickness, from lightweight options for warm water to thicker winter wetsuits for colder conditions.
  • The calculation happens in your browser. The temperature you enter is not sent to our server or stored.

What it doesn't do

This website provides general wetsuit recommendations based on water temperature. It does not take into account every factor that can affect how warm you feel, such as air temperature, wind, currents, session length, wetsuit fit or individual cold tolerance.

The recommendations are intended as a general guide, not a guarantee of comfort or protection against cold. Different wetsuit manufacturers may also provide slightly different temperature recommendations for their products.

The calculator does not replace your own judgement when assessing water conditions or deciding whether it is safe to enter the water. Always consider local conditions, your experience and the suitability of your equipment. The results are provided for general information and are not professional or safety advice.
